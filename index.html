Multimedia Signal Processing Toolkit

A collection of computer-vision, lossless audio-coding, multimedia-compliance and intelligent-media projects. The main showcase is a CCTV traffic-analysis system that detects, tracks and counts moving vehicles from video.

Watch the vehicle-tracking demo View source on GitHub

Traffic Vehicle Detection, Tracking & Counting

OpenCV-based motion analysis identifies moving vehicles in CCTV footage, cleans foreground masks, filters detections, tracks objects between frames and estimates direction for counting.

  • Road-region masking
  • MOG2 background subtraction
  • Frame differencing
  • Morphological mask cleanup
  • Contour and bounding-box filtering
  • Fragmented-detection merging
  • Overlap and centroid-based tracking
  • Direction estimation and vehicle counting
